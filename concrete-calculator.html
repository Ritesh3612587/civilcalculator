Technical Guide: Concrete Volume & Mix Mechanics

1. Geometric Volume Formulation

All inputs are converted to SI meters prior to multiplication to eliminate unit conversion errors. For a rectangular prism of length $L$, width $W$, and depth $D$:

V_wet = (L × W × D) × N × (1 + wastage / 100)

For circular elements, area equals $\pi \times (D/2)^2$, multiplied by height $H$.

2. Dry Volume Multiplier (1.54 Factor)

When dry cement, sand, and stone are mixed with water, sand grains fill the void spaces between aggregate pieces, reducing dry bulk volume by roughly 30% to 35%. Therefore, civil engineering standards require a factor of 1.52 to 1.57 (nominally 1.54) dry material for every cubic meter of hardened wet concrete.

V_dry = V_wet × 1.54
Cement (kg) = (Cement Ratio / Sum of Ratios) × V_dry × 1440 kg/m³
