Mathematical Derivation of the d² / 162 Formula

The empirical civil engineering formula $W = d^2 / 162$ is derived directly from the fundamental physics of a cylindrical steel bar with nominal density $\rho = 7850\text{ kg/m}^3$:

Volume of 1 meter bar = π × (d / 2000)² × 1 = (π / 4,000,000) × d² m³
Weight = Volume × Density = (π / 4,000,000) × d² × 7850 kg
Weight = (7850 × 3.14159265 / 4,000,000) × d² = 0.00616537 × d²
Weight = d² / (1 / 0.00616537) = d² / 162.197 ≈ d² / 162.28 kg/m
