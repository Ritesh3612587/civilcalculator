Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Ownership of Project Documents

You retain 100% intellectual property ownership of all architectural blueprints, structural calculation notes, bill of quantities (BOQ), and project specifications uploaded to BuildScope AI. We do not use your proprietary project drawings to train general public foundation models.

2. Security & Encryption Standards

All file transfers are encrypted with TLS 1.3 in transit. At rest, documents and extracted vector chunks are encrypted using AES-256 in private object storage. Document downloads are served strictly through short-lived signed URLs generated only after verifying your authenticated project membership.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

When a project or document is deleted by an authorized workspace owner, all source files, cached text chunks, embeddings, and generated takeoff items are irrevocably purged from active storage within 30 days.

4. Public Calculators Anonymity

Calculations executed on our public web calculators (Concrete, Steel Bar Weight, Beam Analysis) run client-side in your browser. We do not log or store private dimension values without your explicit direction.
