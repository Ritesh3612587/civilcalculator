Governing Equilibrium Equations for Simply Supported Beams

1. Point Load (P) at Distance (a)

Taking moment about support B ($\sum M_B = 0$):

RA = P × (L - a) / L
RB = P × a / L
M_max = (P × a × (L - a)) / L (at x = a)

2. Full-Span Uniform Load (UDL, w)

By symmetry and vertical equilibrium ($\sum F_y = 0$):

RA = RB = w × L / 2
M(x) = (w × L × x / 2) - (w × x² / 2)
M_max = w × L² / 8 (at midspan x = L/2)
