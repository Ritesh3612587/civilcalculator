BuildScope AI Appendix A Formal Notice:
BuildScope AI provides calculation and document-analysis tools to support engineering workflows. Results depend on user inputs, source-document quality, assumptions, and implemented methods. Outputs may contain errors or omissions and are not a substitute for applicable codes, project specifications, independent verification, or review by a qualified professional. Users are responsible for checking inputs, assumptions, calculations, and final project decisions. Do not use unreviewed outputs as the sole basis for safety-critical design, construction, or compliance decisions.

1. Verification by Licensed Engineers

All computations generated by BuildScope AI—including but not limited to concrete volume estimates, mix proportions, rebar weight schedules, beam bending moments, shear forces, and drawing takeoff measurements—are intended solely as preliminary drafting and estimation aids. They do not constitute certified structural engineering drawings, stamped designs, or statutory regulatory submissions.

2. AI Grounding & Limitations

While our AI document intelligence implements strict citation grounding, large language models and optical character recognition (OCR) algorithms may occasionally fail to identify contradictory drawing revisions, handwritten annotations, or obscure general notes. The user retains sole professional responsibility for confirming cited clauses directly against original contract drawings.

3. Building Codes & Regional Jurisdictions

Calculations are formulated against nominal principles of mechanics and standard codes (such as IS 456, IS 1786, BS 8110, and ACI 318). Regional amendments, seismic zone multipliers, wind load criteria, and geotechnical soil reports must always be incorporated by the engineer of record.
